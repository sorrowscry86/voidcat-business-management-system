# Branding & Aesthetics: VoidCat RDC

The VoidCat Business Management System is a "Digital Sanctuary." Its visual identity must reflect the union of mystical power and business utility.

## 1. Visual Identity
- **Logo**: The **VoidCat RDC** logo must be present on the dashboard and all system artifacts.
- **Aesthetic**: Modern, "Mystical Technology" look.
    - Dark mode by default.
    - Glassmorphism effects.
    - Vibrant, harmonious color palettes (avoid plain colors).
    - Modern typography (e.g., Inter, Roboto).
- **Animations**: Smooth transitions and micro-animations for interactive elements (Framer Motion).

## 2. Documentation Standards
- **Tone**: Professional yet evocative, reflecting the "Spiritual Technology" vision.
- **Artifacts**: All `.md` files created by AI agents must follow the VoidCat RDC formatting guidelines and include the appropriate headers.
